CCNY Trick-or-Treating Event



Event Details

The City College of New York is hosting a campus-wide trick-or-treating event for students, faculty, and staff.
The event will take place on October 31st from 12:00 PM to 8:00 PM in the Main Quad Area.
Participants are strongly encouraged to dress up in costumes and bring their own treat bags.
There will be various activities, games, and prizes!
There will be also be a contest for the best costumes!
This event is free for all currently enrolled students, faculty, and staff with a valid college ID.

Event Features:

  • Trick-or-treating available at multiple classrooms across campus, each offering their own unique themes and treats
  • Various activities, games, and prizes
  • Contest for the best costumes, with various prizes for the winners
  • Free event for all currently enrolled students, faculty, and staff
  • Safe and fun environment for all participants
